**Exact definitions** (same source, Note 1): Total ARR = annualized value of all *active contracts* at
period end, including recurring subscription offerings, **customer support attached to both perpetual and
term licenses**, enhanced support, and managed services; excludes perpetual licenses and professional
services. Term arrangements annualized as (total active contract value ÷ contract days) × 365;
consumption/pay-as-you-go annualized from a prior month's actual usage. **Subscription ARR** = the portion
attributable to term licenses, maintenance/support on term licenses, SaaS, and consumption arrangements.
**SaaS ARR** = the cloud-hosted portion of Subscription ARR. Note that Commvault's ARR includes
perpetual-license support, which most peers exclude — it is *not* comparable to Rubrik's Subscription ARR.

### ⚠️ Definitional recasts effective FY27 — the biggest forecasting trap in this pack

Both changes are in the "Recast Historical Financial Results" section of the
[Q4 FY26 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1169561/000116956126000013/q4fy26pressrelease.htm):

1. **Customer support is being split into "Term-based support" and "Perpetual support," and term-based
   support moves INTO total subscription revenue.** Total revenue and total cost of revenue are unchanged.
   Recast FY26 total subscription = **$970,468K** vs. $768,305K as originally reported — a **+$202,163K
   (+26%) step-up in the subscription line with no change to total revenue**. Recast FY25 total
   subscription = $758,875K (vs. $589,667K). Recast quarterly subscription: Q1'26 $229,309K, Q2'26
   $222,351K, Q3'26 $257,291K, Q4'26 $261,517K.
2. **Subscription ARR is being recast to include enterprise support** (previously only in Total ARR).
   Total ARR unchanged. Recast Subscription ARR: Q4'26 **$1,014,729K** (vs. $989,294K reported); Q1'26
   $867,306K; Q4'25 $802,390K.

Consequence: FY27 guidance of $1,115–1,125M subscription revenue is **~+15–16% growth off the recast
$970.5M base**, not ~+45% off the $768.3M as-reported base. Same for Subscription ARR guidance
($1,200–1,210M vs. recast $1,014.7M ≈ +18–19%).

**Other comparability notes:** Commvault issued **$880,863K of convertible notes in FY26** (nil at
3/31/25), and cash rose to $900M from $302M; FY26 includes a Tinton Falls HQ sale-leaseback (completed
4/28/25, $36M cash) whose adjustments are excluded from non-GAAP; Satori Cyber closed 8/28/25 and
Clumio-related acquisitions affect SaaS ARR (acquired SaaS ARR excluded from NRR for ~12 months); a
cost-optimization/restructuring plan started in Q3 FY26.

### Seasonality

Q3 and Q4 are the strongest, Q1/Q2 weakest, though FY26 broke the pattern with Q3 > Q4. Total revenue by
quarter: FY24 Q2 $201.0M → Q3 $216.8M → Q4 $223.3M (FY24 total $839.2M); FY25 $224.7M / $233.3M / $262.6M
/ $275.0M; FY26 $282.0M / $276.2M / $313.8M / $311.7M. Q2 FY26 declined sequentially — term-license

### ⚠️ Comparability traps

1. **Material rights (Subscription Credits).** Rubrik granted customers credits in exchange for
   relinquishing free next-gen appliance "Refresh Rights"; these are ASC 606 customer options accounted
   for as material rights, and **deferred revenue on forfeited options is released into revenue at
   forfeiture**. Material-rights revenue: **$70.2M in FY26**, **$18.0M in Q4 FY26**, **$13.4M in Q1 FY26**,
   **$8.5M in Q1 FY27**. Management guides that the benefit continues through FY27 but "significantly
   reduce[s] sequentially." Revenue excluding material rights grew 43% in Q1 FY27 vs. 39% reported —
   **reported growth is currently understated** by the roll-off, the opposite of the usual direction.
2. **Hybrid-cloud RSC single-performance-obligation judgment.** Rubrik concluded the cloud service and
   on-prem software licenses in an RSC hybrid subscription are *not distinct*, so the whole bundle is
   recognized **ratably** — unlike Commvault, which recognizes term-license software largely upfront. This
   is why Rubrik has ~97% ratable subscription revenue and Commvault has lumpy term-license quarters. Do
   not model the two the same way.
3. **Appliance transition.** Appliance sales shifted from Rubrik to contract manufacturers (largely
   complete in FY25) and the RSC transition completed in FY26 — this is why "Other revenue" *shrank* 10%
   in FY26.
4. **Rubrik guides no operating margin.** It guides **Subscription ARR Contribution Margin** =
   (Subscription ARR − TTM non-GAAP subscription COGS − TTM non-GAAP opex) ÷ Subscription ARR. It mixes a
   point-in-time balance with trailing expenses and is **not** an operating margin. Q1 FY27: 13.2%; Q4
   FY26: 11.6%.
5. **FY25 SBC distortion:** $913.9M of SBC in FY25 vs. $329.4M in FY26 from IPO-related vesting; GAAP
   margins across FY25 are unusable as a run-rate.

### Seasonality

Q4 is strongest, Q1 weakest, with clean sequential growth every quarter. Revenue: FY25 $187.3M / $205.0M /
$236.2M / $258.1M; FY26 $278.5M / $309.9M / $350.2M / $377.7M; Q1 FY27 $387.1M. Net-new Subscription ARR
is more seasonal than revenue — Q4 FY26 set a record $115M vs. ~$103M in Q1 FY27.

## Time-series comparability warnings

1. **Commvault FY27 subscription-revenue recast (largest trap).** Term-based support moves into
   subscription revenue starting FY27, lifting recast FY26 subscription revenue from $768.3M to $970.5M
   (+$202.2M) with **no change to total revenue**. Any model comparing FY27 guidance ($1,115–1,125M) to
   as-reported FY26 subscription revenue will overstate growth by ~30 percentage points.
2. **Commvault FY27 Subscription ARR recast.** Enterprise support moves from Total-ARR-only into
   Subscription ARR: recast Q4 FY26 Subscription ARR is $1,014.7M vs. $989.3M reported. Total ARR is
   unaffected.
3. **Commvault ARR is structurally broader than Rubrik's.** Commvault's Total ARR includes support attached
   to *perpetual* licenses; Rubrik's Subscription ARR explicitly excludes perpetual maintenance. Never
   place these side by side as like-for-like recurring revenue.
4. **Commvault's SaaS NRR has no history before Q2 FY26** (first disclosed October 28, 2025), is
   **SaaS-only**, is **constant-currency at March 2025 rates**, and **excludes acquired SaaS ARR for ~12
   months** post-close.
5. **Rubrik's material rights inflate then deflate reported revenue.** $70.2M in FY26, $18.0M in Q4 FY26,
   $8.5M in Q1 FY27, guided to decline sequentially through FY27. Reported revenue growth is currently
   *lower* than underlying growth; extrapolating reported growth rates forward will understate the
   underlying business, then overstate it once the roll-off ends.
6. **Rubrik's revenue-recognition model differs fundamentally from Commvault's.** Rubrik treats hybrid RSC
   cloud + on-prem software as a single, non-distinct performance obligation recognized ratably; Commvault
   recognizes term-license software largely at a point in time. Commvault's quarterly term-license line is
   therefore volatile (Q1 FY26 $109.3M → Q2 FY26 $92.6M → Q3 FY26 $119.0M), the direct cause of its only
   recent guidance miss.
7. **Rubrik guides Subscription ARR Contribution Margin, not operating margin.** It divides a point-in-time
   ARR balance by TTM non-GAAP costs. Not comparable to Commvault's non-GAAP EBIT margin, nor to any GAAP
   measure.
8. **Rubrik FY25 GAAP results are distorted by $913.9M of IPO-related SBC** (vs. $329.4M FY26); GAAP gross
   margin also jumped from 70.0% to 80.1% partly from SBC in cost of revenue falling from $67.0M to $18.9M.
9. **Both companies now carry convertible notes issued mid-window** (Commvault $880.9M in FY26; Rubrik 2030
   notes issued June 2025), changing interest lines, diluted-share counts and FCF comparability.
10. **FX is material for Commvault and immaterial-to-unstated for Rubrik.** Commvault's FY26 reported
    revenue growth of 19% was 16% in constant currency, and reported net-new ARR diverges sharply from
    constant-currency net-new ARR in individual quarters (Q1 FY26: $66.2M reported vs. $39.6M CC). Always
    use the constant-currency ARR series for trend work.
11. **Restructuring at Commvault** began in Q3 FY26 and pulled FY26 FCF guidance down $10–15M for one-time
    payments; FY26 SBC differs by $4.5M depending on whether restructuring-related SBC is included.
12. **Cohesity's $1.5B ARR / $1.7B revenue are *pro forma adjusted* for a fiscal year ending July 2024** —
    they combine two companies for a period before the merger closed, and are not audited actuals of the
    merged entity. They also predate the Arctera carve-out being sold onward to Cloud Software Group.
